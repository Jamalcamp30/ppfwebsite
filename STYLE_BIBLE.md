# PPF Athletics — Style Bible

## Reference extraction

### EXOS — clarity and system credibility
- Lead with a plain, consequential promise instead of decorative copy.
- Organize multiple audiences under one understandable performance system.
- Use proof, outcomes, and human coaching language to carry authority.
- Keep navigation and conversion actions clear even when the offering is broad.

### DOGPOUND — image-led confidence
- Let athletes and environment establish the brand before explanatory copy does.
- Use large type, direct labels, and restrained supporting text.
- Treat membership and training as an identity, not a list of amenities.
- Keep the palette disciplined and the interaction physical.

### BXR — premium restraint
- Use space, pacing, and editorial scale to signal quality.
- Make the primary inquiry action continuously easy to find.
- Present the gym as an ecosystem: training, recovery, expertise, and community.
- Avoid filling every viewport; one message should dominate at a time.

## PPF translation

### Mood
Industrial, disciplined, cinematic, human. The site should feel like entering a serious training room before the first rep: quiet tension, visible standards, then controlled intensity.

### Typography
- Display: Archivo Black, uppercase, compressed spacing, short lines.
- Body: Inter, high legibility, moderate line length.
- Orange is reserved for proof, action, and chapter orientation.

### Motion
- Motion explains hierarchy; it never decorates empty space.
- Hero media slowly advances as the user scrolls.
- Sections reveal once, with distance and timing based on reading order.
- The method progress rail shows where the visitor is in the process.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete experience without animation.

### Pacing
- Every major section is at least one viewport tall.
- Each chapter answers one question: What is PPF? Who is it for? How does it work? Where is the proof? Who leads it? How do I begin?
- Dense data appears only after the emotional and strategic case is established.

### Interaction
- “Start your plan” remains one glance away.
- The athlete board is filterable but does not become a dashboard gimmick.
- Mobile navigation is full-screen and thumb-friendly.

## House rules
- No template hero followed by three generic cards.
- No stock gradients.
- Motion must carry story or state.
- Every section earns its scroll.
- No dependency unless the experience cannot be achieved with native HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Acceptance bar
A working designer should not identify the page as an AI template from layout, rhythm, typography, component repetition, or motion behavior.

### Objective checks
- Persistent conversion action on desktop and mobile.
- All core chapters use `min-height: 100svh` or deliberate long-form scroll structure.
- No framework or animation library.
- Keyboard-operable navigation, filters, links, and form.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Responsive at 360px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px.
- Existing PPF proof, contact details, application delivery, and draft-board anchor retained.
