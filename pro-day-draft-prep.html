02 / THE SYSTEM

Evaluation architecture

Every number has
a reason behind it.

The PPF system connects each training decision to the profile an athlete needs to present. Movement quality, physical output and testing execution are developed together.

01

Profile assessment

Establish the athlete’s current numbers, movement limitations, position demands, body composition, testing calendar and the benchmarks that will change the evaluation.

02

Speed architecture

Build acceleration angles, first-step efficiency, split consistency, transition mechanics and top-end velocity without separating technique from usable force.

03

Power conversion

Develop strength that transfers into vertical jump, broad jump, starts, change of direction, position movement and repeatable output under pressure.

04

Testing rehearsal

Rehearse the order, recovery, commands, setup, pacing and decision-making of the actual evaluation so testing day feels familiar instead of improvised.

05 / PREPARATION CYCLE

From arrival to evaluation

A preparation cycle
with no missing phase.

The calendar changes by athlete, but the logic does not. Establish the profile, build the capacity, rehearse the event and verify readiness before the athlete enters the room.

01

Baseline

Testing, film context, movement review, body composition, injury history, position demands and target benchmarks.

02

Build

Speed, strength, explosive power, conditioning, mobility and position work developed around the athlete’s profile.

03

Rehearse

Full testing sequences, recovery windows, commands, transitions, position drills and evaluation-day pacing.

04

Verify

Track the numbers, confirm repeatability, adjust the final details and communicate the athlete’s strongest evidence clearly.

05

Arrive ready

The athlete enters Pro Day, camp or private evaluation knowing the sequence, the standards and the profile they are prepared to present.
